Draplet 0.5.0 Alpha

  • Hide and reveal both sides of the menu bar, with matching indicators and independent click/hover behavior.
  • Curtain and Fade animations, plus Hidden Bar mode on the right.
  • A compact Pocket for overflow and pinned status icons. Hold Command to drag; ordinary clicks open menus.
  • Cached or Live background matching, native light/dark Settings and configurable shortcuts.
  • Automatic signed updates: download in the background, install on quit, or Update and Relaunch.
  • Fullscreen and Mission Control suppression, safer native input routing and last-good icon caching.

Early alpha, build 45. Current hardware validation: Apple silicon, macOS 27. The arm64 app targets macOS 14.2; older systems and multiple displays still need validation.

Not Developer ID signed or notarized. Initial launch may require Open Anyway in macOS Privacy & Security. Do not disable Gatekeeper globally.

Some returned extras use a compatibility slot with a cached real glyph; changing artwork needs Refresh Icons. Arbitrary custom publisher panels and physical Command-drag need broader testing.
